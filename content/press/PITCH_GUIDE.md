# How to Pitch Abraxas — Press & Industry Guide

Quality over spray. Prioritize outlets and journalists who cover **infrastructure, real assets, and verification** — not generic token launch cycles.

## Story angles (pick one per pitch)

1. **Infrastructure:** "The Stripe for reusable trust" — verification that compounds across RWAs
2. **Problem-first:** Repeated verification as hidden tax on institutional RWA adoption
3. **Proof:** Cielo Sunrise genesis case — live STR, $1.1M appraisal, USDC on Sui, Superhost
4. **Partners:** Design partners in final execution (name only when approved)
5. **Tribal / mineral:** High-stakes document reuse for land and mineral rights (when cleared for public)

## Tier 1 — Crypto infrastructure & RWAs

- CoinDesk (policy / infrastructure desks)
- The Block
- Decrypt
- Cointelegraph (infrastructure beat, not price)
- CryptoSlate
- Bitcoin.com News
- Crypto.news
- The Defiant

**Pitch:** Lead with verification infrastructure, not token. Attach Cielo case study link and registry URL.

## Tier 2 — Real assets & finance

- Real estate finance trade press (collateral, STR operators)
- Private credit / fintech infrastructure newsletters
- Proptech outlets covering verification and compliance

**Pitch:** Quantify repeated verification cost. Offer operator interview.

## Tier 3 — Native American economic development

- Tribal business journals and economic development networks (when tribal pipeline cleared for public)

**Pitch:** Document reuse, sovereignty, generational asset stewardship — only with tribal partner approval.

## What to include in every pitch

- 2-sentence problem thesis
- 1 proof link: /case-studies/cielo
- 1 live link: /#registry or /cielo/verified-rate
- Founder availability for technical or operator interview
- Explicit: "Not an investment offer" if asset metrics mentioned

## What never to include

- Placeholder partner names
- Unverified Superhost / rating claims
- "Live" for integrations still in final onboarding
- Fabricated transaction volume or TVL

## Follow-up cadence

- One initial pitch + one follow-up at 5 business days
- If no fit, move on — do not spray CC lists
- Update journalists when **external relying party** hits production (real milestone)

## Assets to attach

- /case-studies/cielo (PDF export if needed)
- /blog/cielo-sunrise-proof-model-works
- Founder headshot + World Labs logo (when available)
- Partner logo only with written approval
