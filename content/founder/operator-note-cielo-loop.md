## The job

Make verification boring. A guest should sign in, approve what gets shared, and move on — without uploading the same ID to every counterparty.

## What shipped

- **Passport** — Google → zkLogin → Sui wallet. Profile, Wallets, Verification, Access tabs. Consent receipts deduped.
- **Cielo verified-rate flow** — reference loop for guest eligibility (pilot, not payment handoff)
- **USDC on Sui** — stablecoin settlement rail for Cielo booking (pilot)
- **Public registry** — browse Cielo, Smyrna, Naj without login; assurance L1–L4 with honest scope labels
- **Site truth pass** — fixed registry images, SSR fallbacks, partner language aligned to reality

## What we learned

1. **Photos and test data destroy credibility faster than weak copy.** A wrong property image on a "verified" card ends the conversation.
2. **Dashboard language kills the ten-second pitch.** Hide complexity until the user is signed in.
3. **Partners are here — names come after approval.** Say "final onboarding" until legal clears public use.

## What's next

- Close first external relying party in production with measured metrics
- Tribal land / mineral pipeline — same trust engine, higher-stakes documents
- Publish partner case study when numbers and names are approved
