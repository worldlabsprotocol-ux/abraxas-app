## Stripe did not win on better databases

Stripe won because **payments got easier every time someone integrated**. Plaid won on bank connectivity. Twilio on communications.

RWAs need the same compounding layer for **trust**.

## The mistake we keep making

Teams lead with tokenization. Institutions ask: "Who verified this? Can I rely on that verification tomorrow? On my platform?"

If the answer is "upload again," the token is irrelevant.

## Reusable trust compounds

Every verification should make the **next** transaction cheaper:

- Fewer documents collected
- Faster approvals
- Lower support burden
- Stronger audit trail

That is network effect — not more passports, **more reusable proof**.

## Abraxas in one line

**Verify once. Transact everywhere.**

Passport is the UX. Policy engine is the API. Registry is the asset record. Cielo is the proof it works.
