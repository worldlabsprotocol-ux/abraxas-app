## Why Cielo exists

Cielo Sunrise is not a demo listing. It is a **live short-term rental** in Mineral Bluff, Georgia — owned and operated by World Labs Protocol, listed on Airbnb, and verified through the same Abraxas pipeline external issuers and relying parties will use.

We eat our own dogfood because investors and partners should not imagine the future. They should **inspect it**.

## Problem we solved

Hospitality operators verify guests repeatedly — platform KYC, direct booking, partner channels, insurance, credit products. Sensitive documents pile up. Drop-off rises. Trust does not compound.

## Solution: Abraxas verification pipeline

1. **Registry** — asset published with assurance taxonomy L1–L4
2. **Passport** — guest signs in with Google (zkLogin)
3. **Consent** — approve minimum proof; partner sees eligibility only
4. **Settlement** — USDC on Sui (pilot rail)
5. **Reuse** — any partner checks ABX-RE-HOSP-001 or guest credentials via policy API

## What this proves

- **Verification before tokenization** — trust creates the asset narrative, not the reverse
- **Real numbers, honest labels** — every metric has date, level, and refresh window
- **Operational proof** — live Airbnb listing bookable independently of Abraxas
- **Technical proof** — zkLogin, credentials, policy decisions, on-chain settlement path
- **Reusable primitive** — same engine partners integrate; Cielo is reference implementation

## What this does not prove yet

- Third-party relying party at full production scale (partners in final onboarding)
- Guaranteed investment returns or token liquidity
- Every vertical (tribal/mineral, private credit) — hospitality first by design
