# Willingness-to-pay research bank (internal)

**Not for first calls.** Use [WILLINGNESS_TO_PAY_INTERVIEW_INTRO_v1.md](./WILLINGNESS_TO_PAY_INTERVIEW_INTRO_v1.md) for intro meetings. Pick follow-ups from this bank as needed.

Do not record wallet addresses, government IDs, or holder PII in written notes.

---

## Opening

1. What does your organization operate (product category, geography, user type)?
2. Who owns verification or access gating decisions today (team and title)?

## Current workflow

3. Walk through the last time you had to verify someone before granting access or completing a transaction. What systems were involved?
4. What breaks or slows that process today?
5. What does a failed or disputed verification cost you?

## Trust and data

6. What evidence do you require to treat a verification as authoritative in **your** systems?
7. Where is that evidence stored, and who can access it?
8. What identity data do you collect that you would prefer not to re-collect on every visit?

## Volume and economics

9. Rough order of magnitude: verifications per month.
10. What do you pay today for verification-related tooling or manual review?
11. How is that budget approved?

## Integration

12. Who would implement a new verification integration?
13. What environments must you support?
14. What security or compliance reviews are required before a new vendor?

## Decision process

15. What would need to be true for a **90-day scoped pilot**?
16. Who must sign off on a paid pilot?
17. What would make you stop a pilot early?

## Pricing discovery (non-leading)

18. What pricing shapes have you accepted before (subscription, usage, hybrid)?
19. What fee range would be too cheap to trust vs too expensive to trial?
20. Would you pay for sandbox testing, production outcomes only, or a fixed pilot fee?

## Close

21. What question should we have asked that we did not?
22. May we follow up with a one-page pilot scope for feedback (no commitment)?
