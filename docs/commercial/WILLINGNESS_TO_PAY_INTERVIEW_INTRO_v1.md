# Institutional intro conversation (6–8 questions)

**Use for a first 30-minute call.** Let the prospect talk. Do not pitch Abraxas until you understand their workflow.

Extended research bank (internal): [WILLINGNESS_TO_PAY_RESEARCH_BANK_v1.md](./WILLINGNESS_TO_PAY_RESEARCH_BANK_v1.md)

---

1. **Workflow:** Walk me through how you decide whether someone is allowed to proceed today — systems, teams, and where the final allow/deny happens.

2. **Cost:** What does that process cost you (vendor fees, engineering, manual review, fraud/chargebacks, support) even roughly?

3. **Failures:** When verification fails or is disputed, what happens next — and how often does that matter?

4. **Independence:** What evidence would your **own** systems need to trust a verification outcome without calling a vendor on every request?

5. **Ownership:** Who would integrate a new verification path (team/title), and who signs off for security or compliance?

6. **Volume:** Order of magnitude verifications per month (band is fine).

7. **Pilot criteria:** What would need to be true to run a **90-day scoped pilot** — and who approves budget for it?

8. **Stop conditions:** What would make you end a pilot early?

---

**After the call (outside git):** problem severity, budget signal, pilot interest, and whether they need independent enforce (not hosted UI). Do not mark commercial gates validated in source control without real evidence.
