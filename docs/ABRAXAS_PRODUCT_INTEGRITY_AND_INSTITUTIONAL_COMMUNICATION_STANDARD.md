# Abraxas Product Integrity and Institutional Communication Standard

**Status: Permanent**

Applies to all Abraxas public, product, developer, institutional, administrative, and partner-facing surfaces.

## 1. Functional integrity

No dead interactions.

Every button, card, tab, menu, dropdown, CTA, and interactive visual control must perform a real action.

If an element is informational, it must not be presented as an actionable button.

No fake controls, placeholder interactions, empty links, misleading status animations, or decorative actions that appear functional.

Every workflow must have a defined next step, success state, failure state, and recovery path where applicable.

All interactions must be tested on desktop and mobile.

## 2. Institutional positioning

Abraxas is institutional trust and verification infrastructure.

All product copy must reflect this established positioning.

Communicate capabilities directly, with clear technical substance and confidence.

Do not introduce speculative or unnecessarily negative commentary such as:

"Not institutionally ready."

"Only a prototype."

"Just a sandbox demo."

"Not suitable for institutions."

"Not real infrastructure."

"Still experimental."

Do not describe mature, implemented capabilities as speculative merely because unrelated Production access or operational prerequisites remain pending.

## 3. Accurate customer communication

Do not fabricate readiness, availability, customers, credentials, security assurances, revenue, or successful verification events.

Keep required security and operational information accurate.

Communicate actionable conditions instead of broad negative judgments.

Examples:

"Production access requires approval."

"Configure issuer trust."

"Request SDK access."

"Complete integration setup."

"Verification is temporarily unavailable. Retry when service is restored."

"Authorization requires current revocation evidence."

Keep explicit fail-closed errors wherever operational correctness, legal obligations, or security require them.

A missing capability must never be presented as available.

## 4. Separate internal diagnostics from product positioning

Engineering readiness evaluations, CI failures, deployment blockers, missing secrets, and internal testing limitations belong in appropriate engineering and operator surfaces.

Customer-facing interfaces should communicate the current product state, required action, and expected outcome.

Do not expose internal build numbers, development commentary, speculative readiness assessments, or infrastructure implementation details unless relevant to a technical integration task.

Do not confuse sandbox availability with Production authorization.

## 5. Product maturity

Abraxas should behave and communicate like infrastructure designed for institutional adoption.

Every release must improve or preserve:

Functionality.

Security.

Independent verification.

Operational reliability.

Visual quality.

Customer understanding.

Institutional credibility.

No visual polish may disguise an incomplete integration.

No engineering transparency should unnecessarily diminish real shipped capability.

## Final rule

**Every interaction must work. Every status must be accurate. Every sentence must strengthen understanding of what Abraxas does.**

Institutional confidence comes from verifiable capability and disciplined execution, not exaggerated claims or unnecessary disclaimers.
